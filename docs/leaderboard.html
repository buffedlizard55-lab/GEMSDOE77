Correction — this is not a 28-raster organizer receipt ledger

The previous version of this page incorrectly described a family-assembled table as 28 organizer-confirmed TIFF submissions, attributed scores to specific GEMSDOE files without matching submission receipts, and included the stale 0.3195 figure as a current top score. Those assertions are withdrawn. A leaderboard row identifies a participant and a score; it does not expose the TIFF filename or checksum in the public page.

Do not use the historical table, its “team ranking,” its file-level scores, or its causal/geologic explanations as verified evidence. The underlying revision history remains in Git for audit.

Metric mechanism: plausible, not proven

The official task describes distance-tolerant scoring with a 300 m triangular kernel. Sparse, well-spaced points can reduce redundant prediction mass; removing points close to already mapped faults can help if those points contribute little credit against the newly mapped target faults. This is a plausible explanation for why the reported H33 construction might work well. Raster forensics and metric algebra do not establish causation, nor do they authenticate the score-to-file mapping.

Evidence classes used in this repository

  • ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED: a score shown on the official leaderboard is confirmed for its displayed participant row, or a submission-page receipt confirms that exact upload.
  • OWNER-REPORT: a user/owner-supplied claim that a specific TIFF received a score, absent a receipt connecting the bytes to the upload.
  • HOLDOUT-DTI: local hide-and-recover result. It is never a leaderboard score and must include evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% CI.
