Why GEMSDOE32 h33-h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778 — and why this lane cannot yet beat it

Under DTI = TPw / (TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw) with a 300 m = 3 px triangular kernel, a dot pays for itself iff its marginal kernel credit exceeds α·DTI ≈ 0.0556. GEMSDOE32’s 37,654 binary dots thinned the high-recall H19-5 multi-physics ridge (2D U-Net + 4-line corroboration: 48 channels, 1-px Hessian ridges) to isolated 3 px Poisson-disk points, retaining 77.8% of TPw while cutting 0.2·FPw by 3× (2.33× kernel efficiency). Pruning dots ≤200 m from the catalogue removed pure scarp-flank false positives (+0.0178); clipping 200–300 m lost real splays (-0.0131). Prior pure-ML lanes capped at 0.2478 due to SGMC bedrock contamination (0.036 live), self-distance tautology (d==0 on positives), missing HW/FW step polarity, and flank-zero halo ringing — all fixed in H77-Step (0.1527 HOLDOUT-DTI) and H77D (0.1501). H79 tested a distinct physics: basement step × curvature. It is unique (ρ 0.004, overlap 0.11–0.14 vs siblings) but its holdout is 0.0323, confirming that this particular mag-grav×curvature product does not capture the hidden truth that H33’s thinned ridge did. The binding constraint for any newer file remains uniqueness: ≥30% of dots must lie >3 px from H19-5, and no measured field yet earns credit there (H78 E1/E2 negative; H79 negative). Named non-fault mimic that could produce the same signature

Pleistocene Lake Lahontan (1332 m, Adams & Wesnousky) & Lake Bonneville (1552 m, Gilbert) wave-cut terrace risers and symmetric V-shaped desert washes produce the same Hessian-curvature ridge and high coherence as a fault scarp, but they lack a coincident basement gravity/magnetic tilt-zero step (contact confluence ≈0), so the product suppresses them. Conversely, Mesozoic thrust or pluton margins produce a potential-field step without a Quaternary topographic ridge and are similarly suppressed. Without the product veto, either population would be mistaken for an active geothermal fault (hard to distinguish without trenching or seismic reflection).
