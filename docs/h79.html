Hypothesis and what the data said

Hypothesis. Attribute-conditioned hanging-wall strand prior -- Signed hanging-wall distance field: d_vis(x) * sign(n_HW . (x - x_nearest_visible)), where n_HW is the dip-direction normal of the nearest visible trace (attribute when given; otherwise the lower-det_elev side, i.e. the downthrown block of a normal fault). Plus nearest-visible-trace recency class, slip-rate class, certainty, map scale, and strike-parallel/normal decomposition of the offset.

Mechanism. The in-footprint catalogue is entirely 1:100,000 and 1:250,000 legacy mapping (MEASURED E0: MAPSCALE 100 = 280 traces, 250 = 846; zero 1:10k INGENIOUS lidar traces inside the footprint). A 1:250k line represents a fault ZONE; lidar-era expert mapping adds synthetic/antithetic strands on the downthrown (hanging-wall, basinward) side within ~0.3-3 km and along-strike continuations. Those strands are absent from the catalogue by mapping scale, not by geology.

Named non-fault mimic. Hanging-wall (basinward) alluvial-fan incision banks, fluvial terrace risers and pluvial Lake Lahontan shoreline benches -- all preferentially located on the same downthrown side.

What actually carried the gain. Seed-30 ablations: dropping the two signed hanging-wall channels does not hurt (H79_noHW 0.1695 vs H79_full 0.1645, paired delta -0.0050 CI [-0.012971514063160664, 0.0032380059355985942]); geometry-only context (strike-aware along/across offsets + density) reaches 0.1613; attributes add +0.0032 CI [-0.0049165368421663445, 0.011475633276121854] on top. The hanging-wall mechanism is NOT supported by the catalogue-vs-catalogue holdout (withheld traces lean slightly to the footwall, E1); the strike-aware context geometry is what the holdout rewards.

Measured data facts. The training labels are pixel-identical to the legacy USGS traces (INGENIOUS increment inside the footprint = 0 px; 1,126 traces, 846 at 1:250k and 280 at 1:100k). The topographically inferred downthrown side agrees with the catalogue's DIPDIRECT attribute on 86.1% of 25,482 attributed pixels. Withheld traces lie on the hanging wall of their nearest visible trace 47.9% of the time (attribute) vs 53.2% for negatives — a slight footwall lean inside the catalogue. Full notes: knowledge/03, research/h79_hypotheses.md.
