What explains the reported H33 result?

Prior byte forensics and the retained analysis support a plausible explanation: sparse predictions reduce redundant mass, and pruning predictions near known faults avoids spending mass on catalogue-excluded locations. H33 retains 37,654 positive pixels and removes near-catalogue dots from an earlier backbone. This is not causal proof from a controlled organizer experiment. The supplied 0.2778 is OWNER-REPORT for the named file; this review obtained no submission-page receipt tying that score to those bytes. The supplied leaderboard targets also conflict. No current leaderboard score, improvement forecast, or likelihood of winning is certified here. Hiding catalogue segments is a useful proxy but not identical to discovering truly unmapped faults or geothermal vents.
