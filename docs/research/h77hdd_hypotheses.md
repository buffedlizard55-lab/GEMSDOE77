## 1. PhD-Level Answer: Why `GEMSDOE32` (`h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros`) Achieved `0.2778` (`OWNER-REPORT`) and How to Beat It

### 1.1 Exact Metric Algebra of the `0.1922 -> 0.2477 -> 0.2600 -> 0.2708 -> 0.2778` Progression
The DrivenData DOE GEMS competition evaluates predictions $P \in [0, 1]^{3730 \times 3292}$ (`EPSG:32611`, $100\text{ m}$ grid; [Problem Page 967](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/)) using the **Distance-Weighted Tversky Index (`DTI`)** with $\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$, and a $300\text{ m}$ ($3\text{ px}$) linear triangular distance kernel $K(\delta) = \max(0, 1 - \|\delta\|_2 / 3)$ ([Tversky Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tversky_index); `vendor/gems57/metric.py`):

$$\mathrm{DTI}(P, T) = \frac{\mathrm{TP}_w(P, T)}{\mathrm{TP}_w(P, T) + 0.2\,\mathrm{FP}_w(P, T) + 0.8\,\mathrm{FN}_w(P, T)} = \frac{\mathrm{TP}_w(P, T)}{0.2\,\mathrm{TP}_w(P, T) + 0.2\,\mathrm{FP}_w(P, T) + 0.8\,|T|}.$$

 attribution caveat (`IR-77-04`, `IR-L-02`): On the public leaderboard ([DrivenData Leaderboard](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/leaderboard/)), row `#48` (`extradr19`) has an **`ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`** score of **`0.2778`**, while the overall `#1` leader (`xiaofanhu`) holds **`0.4413`** (`ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`, superseding the older `0.3195` figure in the project brief). The link between `0.2778` and the specific file `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif` in [GEMSDOE32](https://buffedlizard55-lab.github.io/GEMSDOE32/docs/index.html) is **`OWNER-REPORT`**.

Byte-level inspection of the restored `H33` raster (`SHA-256: c55bafc470054e8271dcb89347a17e07fefe50de6af6e6ba6c4b169ef7ab6fa9`, `37,654` binary `1.0` pixels, `0` on mapped catalogue, minimum catalogue distance $\sqrt{5} \approx 2.24\text{ px}$) explains why its emission geometry is near-optimal for a fixed ridge support:
1. **Binary Extreme-Point Optimality (`{0, 1}` vs Continuous Soft Probabilities):** Because $\mathrm{TP}_w$ uses a spatial supremum over the $3\text{ px}$ kernel disk whereas $\mathrm{FP}_w = \sum_x P(x)(1 - D_T(x))$ integrates prediction mass linearly across ~5.1 million background pixels, diffuse continuous probabilities (`6GEMSDOE` `0.0286`, `GEMSDOE4` `0.0343`, `GEMSDOE9` `0.0107` `OWNER-REPORT`) incur massive denominator penalties. Binary `{0, 1}` emission eliminates background probability leakage.
2. **Non-Overlapping $300\text{ m}$ (`d = 2.8–3.0 px`) Kernel Packing (`19GEMSDOE` `0.1922` $\to$ `GEMSDOE25` `0.2600` `OWNER-REPORT`):** Along a 1-D fault trace, every pixel on a solid 1-px ridge (`H19-5`, `121,131` px) pays $0.2$ in false-positive risk, yet adjacent pixels within $<3\text{ px}$ redundantly cover the same $300\text{ m}$ triangular neighborhood. Spacing binary dots by $2.8\text{–}3.0\text{ px}$ cuts total prediction mass by $69\%$ while retaining $\sim 78\text{–}85\%$ of line-integral kernel coverage.
3. **Near-Catalogue Collar Pruning (`d_cat <= 2 px` Removed: `GEMSDOE25` `0.2600` $\to$ `GEMSDOE28` `0.2708` $\to$ `GEMSDOE32` `0.2778` `OWNER-REPORT`):** Known USGS/INGENIOUS faults are masked when scoring hidden faults. Dots placed at $d_{\text{cat}} \le 2\text{ px}$ ($\le 200\text{ m}$) from mapped faults lose most of their $300\text{ m}$ kernel disk to the masked catalogue trace, yielding sub-threshold marginal true-positive return ($\Delta \mathrm{TP}_w < \alpha \cdot \mathrm{DTI} \approx 0.0556$). Removing the $6,436$ dots at $d_{\text{cat}} \le 2\text{ px}$ (`44,090` $\to$ `37,654` dots) pruned zero-return mass without sacrificing unmapped splay faults starting at $d_{\text{cat}} > 2\text{ px}$ (whereas `GEMSDOE33` pruning to $>3\text{ px}$ dropped to `0.2632` `OWNER-REPORT`).
