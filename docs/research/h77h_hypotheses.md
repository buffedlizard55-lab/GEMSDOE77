1. **The Tversky Precision-Penalty Asymmetry ($\alpha = 0.2, \beta = 0.8$) and 300 m Triangular Kernel Packing (`D1.5 -> D2.8`):**
   In the DrivenData Distance-Tolerant IoU (DTI) metric:
   $$\text{DTI}(A, B) = \frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + 0.2\,\text{FP} + 0.8\,\text{FN}}, \quad s_X(p) = \max_{x \in X}\max\!\left(0, 1 - \frac{\|p - x\|_2}{300\text{ m}}\right)$$
   Because each emitted dot $x \in B$ creates a 29-pixel radial coverage cone of radius $R = 3\text{ px}$ ($300\text{ m}$), two dots placed $1\text{ px}$ apart along a candidate fault trace have $>83\%$ overlapping $s_B$ cones: the second dot adds negligible new $\text{TP}$ on true faults while adding almost a full unit of $\text{FP}$ penalty ($0.2\,\text{FP}$) on every false segment!
