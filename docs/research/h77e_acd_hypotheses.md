## Executive Scientific Diagnosis: Why Prior GEMSDOE Lanes Plateaued and How `H77E` Breaks Both Ceilings

Across all prior repositories (`GEMSDOE` #1 through `GEMSDOE77` PR #12), every model encountered three distinct bottlenecks:

1. **Pixel-Wise Tabular Myopia Across Eroded Alluvial Wash Gaps (`Why 2D U-Nets reach 0.4413 ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED while 1-px tabular trees stall`):**
   Every prior tree lane (`H77D`, `H77-Step`, `H77-HSL`, `GEMSDOE70`, `GEMSDOE74`) classified pixel $(r, c)$ using **only scalar features evaluated at $(r, c)$**. Across Quaternary alluvial fans in Nevada, ephemeral desert washes incise through range-front and piedmont scarps every $300\text{–}900\text{ m}$, locally erasing 1 m and 10 m scarp relief (`lidar_ex_max -> 0`, `slope -> 0`). Pixel-wise trees collapse inside every wash crossing and over-fire on wide (`8–15 px`) mountain-front talus blobs. **Two-stage Out-of-Fold Filament Auto-Context (`Tu & Bai 2010`)** solves both problems simultaneously without GPU U-Net overfitting by extracting multi-scale along-strike line integrals, **bilateral wash-gap bridges $\min(\bar{P}_+, \bar{P}_-)$**, and cross-strike flank inhibition strictly from **out-of-fold Stage-1 probability fields** (`0` in-sample leakage).
2. **Unused Physical Scarp Polarity & Tectonic Fluvial Knickpoint Physics (`upface_max`, `downface_max`, `cross_max`, `dem10_chi`):**
   - `H77D` never loaded `lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif` Bands 6 (`downface_max`), 7 (`upface_max`), 8 (`cross_max`), or 2 (`ex_mean`), and used `0` channels from `dem10_chi_100m_i16.tif`.
   - Physically, **uphill-facing scarps (`upface_max`)** and **paired synthetic–antithetic graben couplets ($\sqrt{\text{downface}_\sigma \cdot \text{upface}_\sigma}$)** are diagnostic of active Quaternary piedmont normal-fault grabens (Wallace 1977; Caskey et al. 1996, *BSSA*, 1954 Dixie Valley rupture) because neither stream incision (`cross_max`) nor pluvial Lake Lahontan wave-cut cliffs (`downface_max` along horizontal equipotential contours) can cut an uphill-facing scarp into an alluvial fan!
   - Meanwhile, across bedrock ranges and non-LiDAR volcanic plateaus, active faults manifest as **strike-aligned transient fluvial $\chi$ / $k_{sn}$ knickpoint waves across adjacent catchments** (Whipple & Tucker 1999; Perron & Royden 2013).
3. **Ad-Hoc Rank-Power Surrogate vs Exact Dinkelbach Fractional-DTI + 1-Swap Optimization + Tip-Gated Junction Collar:**
   Prior submodular allocators (`H77D`, `H77-Step`, `GEMSDOE70`) discarded calibrated probabilities in favor of `(rank - q_0)^\gamma`, hard-fixed an arbitrary global dot count `B`, stopped after greedy single-pass insertion (leaving knight's-move / diagonal coverage pockets), and hard-banned `d_vis <= 3.0 px` everywhere (creating a 600 m dead zone at every fault tip where unmapped splays branch from `d_cat = 1..2 px`!). Dividing `DTI = T / (0.2 T + 0.2 F + 0.8 K)` by `T` proves exact equivalence to maximizing $T(D) / (F(D) + 4K)$, solved to fixed-point optimality by **Dinkelbach's fractional programming iteration (Dinkelbach 1967)** + **1-swap local search** + **tip-gated junction collar**.
