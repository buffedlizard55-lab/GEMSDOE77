All candidates use already restored competition/pinned public layers; no new external source is needed. Expected DTI improvement is an ordinal research priority, not a projected score.

| 1 | **Lagged radiometric alteration-halo pair** — GeoDAWN K, Th, U | A selective-U gradient displaced 300 m from a K-Th common-mode edge on either flank; center the prediction on the common edge and penalize zero-lag coincidence. A buried permeable trace can alter/mobile-U soils without a scarp and therefore be missing from the mapped catalogue. | H77-U used same-pixel U-residual Hessian curvature; H77D used broad radiometric channels. | 3 | **Strain–conductivity transfer-zone junction** — geodetic shear/dilation and conductivity surface | High strain-axis rotation collocated with the endpoint, not the length, of a conductivity edge; transfer zones can host fluid flow despite weak surface expression. | Junction/end-point topology rather than H77D's general hydrothermal/tectonic ensemble. | Irrigation-groundwater conductivity boundary. | Medium / medium CPU |
