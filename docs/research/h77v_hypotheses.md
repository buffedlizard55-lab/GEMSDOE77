1. **Exact Distance-Tolerant Tversky (`DTI`) Functional ([DrivenData Problem Description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/), [Tversky 1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tversky_index)):**
   Let $G \subset \Omega$ be the hidden positive fault pixel set on the $3730 \times 3292$ `EPSG:32611` 100 m grid (`5,167,373` valid footprint pixels) and $p \in [0, 1]^\Omega$ be the submitted prediction raster after visible-fault masking ($p(x) = 0$ on known mapped faults). With the linear triangular kernel $k(d) = \max(1 - d / 300\text{ m}, 0)$ ($R = 3\text{ px}$ at 100 m resolution) and spatial dilation operators $D_p(g) = \max_{x \in \Omega} p(x)\,k(d(x, g))$ and $D_G(x) = \max_{g \in G} k(d(x, g))$, the exact score implemented in `vendor/gems57/metric.py` (`α = 0.2, β = 0.8`) is:
   $$\mathrm{DTI}(p, G) = \frac{\mathrm{TP}_w}{\mathrm{TP}_w + 0.2\,\mathrm{FP}_w + 0.8\,\mathrm{FN}_w} = \frac{\sum_{g \in G} D_p(g)}{0.8\,|G| + 0.2\,\sum_{g \in G} D_p(g) + 0.2\,\sum_{x \in \Omega} p(x)\bigl[1 - D_G(x)\bigr]}.$$
   Under `INSTRUMENT` marginal analysis, three geometric laws follow immediately:
   - **Binary Extreme-Point Optimality:** Because $D_p(g)$ takes the **supremum (`max`)** over the 29-pixel disk $\|\delta\|_2 \le 3$ while $\mathrm{FP}_w$ sums $p(x)$ **linearly** across the $\sim 5.05 \times 10^6$ off-fault background pixels, continuous background probabilities ($\bar{p} \in [0.01, 0.10]$) add $10,000\text{–}100,000$ false-positive penalty units to the denominator without increasing $D_p(g)$ above its peak (`6GEMSDOE: 0.0286`, `GEMSDOE4: 0.0343`, `GEMSDOE9: 0.0107` `OWNER-REPORT`).
   - **300 m (`3 px`) Submodular Disk Packing:** Placing isolated binary `1.0` dots spaced by $\sim 2.8\text{–}3.0\text{ px}$ ($280\text{–}300\text{ m}$) along a 1-D fault trace preserves $7/9 \approx 77.8\%$ of continuous line recall $\mathrm{TP}_w$ while reducing false-positive mass $0.2\,\mathrm{FP}_w$ by $\sim 3\times$ (`GEMSDOE25`/`GEMSDOE30` `D2.8`: `0.2600` vs `19GEMSDOE` `H19-5` continuous ridge: `0.1922` `OWNER-REPORT`).
