The comparison below is against the code and research files present in this repository, not every private or public GEMSDOE sibling. Therefore “not found here” is not a global novelty claim. The research target is fault detection; geothermal indicators may be covariates, but a fault raster is not a geothermal-resource or vent confirmation.

## Required gate sequence if a future lane is assigned

1. Resolve Policy I17's dense-witness conflict through a protocol-owner decision and a shared template/registry rule. Do not create a private exemption.
2. Reconfirm a single-method lane assignment; check the full sibling registry before committing a surface or placing dots. A partial scan can reject a candidate but cannot certify uniqueness.
3. For rank 1, restore the pinned DEM inputs and verify their manifest hashes. Record that owner-mirror hashes are not organizer authentication. Derive features only from training layers and fold-visible catalogue features; evaluate each channel alone as a leakage canary.
4. Run the unchanged shared `gems57-pooled-hide-v2` whole-component buffered hide-and-recover evaluator, with pooled DTI, 300 m triangular kernel, and paired block confidence intervals. Compare against a fold-rebuilt incumbent and matched random baseline. Do not treat a projection or another lane's result as this experiment's score.
5. Check correlation and 3-pixel overlap on the surface before placement and on final dots. If either literal threshold fires, stop, log a duplicate, and do not continue.
6. Use the unchanged shared writer, independently re-read the TIFF, validate finite values in [0,1] and exact grid metadata, then record the artifact hash. Format validity is not uniqueness, promotion, portal acceptance, or approval.
7. No competition slot is used unless a separate selector explicitly approves a file after checking the official rules, weekly cap, and a genuine organizer receipt workflow.
