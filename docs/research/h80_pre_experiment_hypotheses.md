## Post-experiment audit — 2026-10-21 (preserves original preregistration)

**Implementation deviation (important):** the frozen `signature` describes a square-root product, while `scripts/h80_build.py` actually computed a cube-root product, blended 50/50 with `depth_persist`. This was discovered after the holdout and registry work. The H80 TIFF and HOLDOUT-DTI therefore measure the implemented **cube-root blend variant**, not the exact square-root signature as preregistered. Do not silently rewrite the registration or transfer the result to the originally specified formula. The three-experiment budget is exhausted, so no correction/re-run was performed; the result remains negative evidence for the variant and does not validate the exact preregistered H80-1 formula. The machine-readable correction is `registry/preregistration_h80_amendment_postrun.json`.

## Post-experiment novelty audit — 2026-10-21 (preserves the original preregistration)

A source/code cross-check after the H80 experiment found that the original H80-4 vent-alignment proposal is **not a novel lane**: existing `src/gems77d/features.py` and `src/gems77_step/features.py` already ingest the GDR 1391 volcanic-vent layer and related geothermal point data. It is therefore disqualified from future implementation; the frozen pre-experiment table above is retained as historical evidence, not edited to hide the screening error. The original H80-2/3 ingredients (earthquake and geodetic bands) also occur in the 19-band training stack and are consumed by broad models; their proposed difference is only a separately testable, explicit spatial-interaction transform, not new input data. This narrows the novelty claim and remains a future candidate, not current evidence.

**Replacement future candidate H80-4R (not preregistered or tested):** sparse borehole thermal-conductivity contrast against mapped depth-to-basement edges. Specific layers: the official [DOE GDR 1390 INGENIOUS Thermal Conductivity Measurement Source Categorization](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1390) (DOI 10.15121/1874222; page states publicly accessible, CC BY 4.0, and links a 907.64 kB XLSX with measured-well metadata/location) plus the cached `depth_to_base_surf` and `cond_surf` bands. Signature: robust local residual of measured borehole thermal conductivity against depth/lithology trends, projected only within a preregistered distance support and intersected with a basement-edge corridor. Rationale is a blind thermal-gradient proxy, not a guaranteed fault or geothermal flow signal. Mimics include lithologic conductivity variation, sparse sampling bias, and drilling-depth selection. **Obtainability was verified on the official GDR landing page; actual file download, coordinates, footprint coverage, and permissible transformation into a raster remain unverified.** It needs a separate source-data license check, a pre-run coverage audit, leakage canaries, and fold-safe holdout before implementation.
