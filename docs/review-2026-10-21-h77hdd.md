1. **Exact DTI Metric Algebra & Breakeven Threshold (`vendor/gems57/metric.py`):**
   For binary predictions $P \in \{0, 1\}$ and hidden ground-truth fault pixels $T \in \{0, 1\}$ on the `3730 × 3292` (`100 m`) grid with a $300\text{ m}$ ($3.0\text{ px}$) radial triangular distance-decay kernel $k(d) = \max(0, 1 - d/300\text{ m})$ and asymmetry weights $\alpha = 0.2$ (false positive), $\beta = 0.8$ (false negative):
   $$\text{DTI} = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{\text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w + 0.8\,\text{FN}_w} = \frac{\sum_{i \in T} c(i)}{0.2\sum_{j \in P} (1 - k_T(j)) + 0.8\,|T| + 0.2\sum_{i \in T} c(i)}$$
   Adding one isolated dot $j \in P$ increases the numerator by $\Delta\text{TP}_w$ (uncovered hidden fault pixels within $3\text{ px}$ weighted by $k$) and increases $\text{FP}_w$ by $1 - k_T(j)$. Thus a candidate dot improves $\text{DTI}$ if and only if its marginal net coverage gain satisfies:
   $$\frac{\Delta\text{TP}_w}{1 - k_T(j)} > \frac{0.2\,\text{DTI}}{1 - 0.2\,\text{DTI}}$$
   At $\text{DTI} = 0.2778$ (`OWNER-REPORT`), the breakeven marginal coverage is **$0.0588$ hidden fault pixel-equivalents per emitted dot**; at `#1` $\text{DTI} = 0.4413$ (`ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`), the breakeven marginal coverage rises to **$0.0968$ hidden fault pixel-equivalents per emitted dot**.
