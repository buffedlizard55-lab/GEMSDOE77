## H80 hypotheses (written before experiment)

The candidate menu and source table are in [`research/h80_pre_experiment_hypotheses.md`](../research/h80_pre_experiment_hypotheses.md), and frozen input/method choices are in [`registry/preregistration_h80.json`](../registry/preregistration_h80.json). 1. **Tested H80-1:** persistent depth-to-basement edge × conductivity edge × gravity edge. Signature: Gaussian gradient magnitude at 1.5/3-pixel scales, rank persistence of basement-depth edge, rank conductivity edge, gravity horizontal-gradient magnitude re-derived from the isostatic gravity surface, fused by geometric/rank blend. Reason it might reveal a buried structure: the target can be under basin fill with no visible scarp; independent subsurface inversion edges could indicate a discontinuity. Mimic: basin/range-front transition without active fault, alluvial lithologic contact, or geophysical survey seam. It did not beat random; no more tuning is authorized.
2. **Future H80-2:** cached earthquake intensity/distance fields × magnetic/gravity edge termination. Potential mimic: unrelated swarm/mining seismicity or acquisition artefacts.
3. **Future H80-3:** geodetic strain and dilatation × detrended elevation profile break. Potential mimic: irrigation subsidence, landslide, or interpolation seams.
4. **Future H80-4R, high cost; replacement after audit, not preregistered:** sparse borehole thermal-conductivity residuals × basement-edge corridor using official GDR 1390. Its public landing page identifies a CC BY 4.0 XLSX download, but spatial coverage and coordinate quality have not been checked. This idea requires a separate lane preregistration before any download-driven implementation.

**Novelty correction:** the original pre-experiment fourth idea (vent alignment × radiometric halo) was not novel: repo code in H77D/H77-Step already uses GDR 1391 volcanic-vent and related geothermal point data. We preserve the original file as an immutable historical preregistration and disqualify that candidate; this is logged as IR-H80-06. The broad raw training stack also includes earthquake and geodetic inputs, so H80-2/H80-3 would be new only as explicit transforms/interactions, not new data layers. This correction limits the claim of a fully screened 3–5 novel-candidate menu; do not reuse those inputs without checking every lane's actual feature use.

H80-1's proposed edge interaction itself is different from a standalone lidar scarp ranker or a catalog-distance detector, but existing H77-Step already uses depth-to-basement and conductivity gradients within its much broader model. Thus novelty is an explicit persistence × cross-sensor interaction and holdout result, not the ingredients. These remain hypotheses, not claims that an official source proves a particular response.
