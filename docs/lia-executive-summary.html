Why the portal said "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"

We measured two mechanisms from the official inputs.

  1. The training_features.tif nodata sentinel (−3.4×10³⁸) appears in 3,061 cells inside the scoring footprint. Left in, these are out-of-range values.
  2. A footprint taken from feature band 1 instead of sample_submission.tif leaves those cells as NaN inside the scored region.

The shared template's own validator uses the band-1 footprint (reported as a template fix in the analysis). This file avoids both mechanisms: no NaN anywhere, no sentinel, and the official footprint.
