Method and competing explanations

H80 computes Gaussian-gradient magnitudes for cached depth-to-basement values at 1.5 and 3 px, rank-persistence across those scales, a conductivity edge, and a gravity horizontal-gradient edge re-derived from the cached isostatic gravity surface. The executed candidate fusion was 0.5 × cube-root(depth persistence × conductivity edge × gravity edge) + 0.5 × depth persistence. The frozen signature had a square root instead; this mismatch is a disclosed protocol deviation. No catalogue-derived predictor or previous submission raster is used. Inputs are pinned owner-supplied mirrors, not organizer-authenticated downloads.

Named non-fault mimics: a basin range-front or sediment-thickness transition without a fault, an alluvial lithologic boundary, and geophysical acquisition/processing seams. The inversion surfaces are smoothed proxies; gradients are not direct borehole measurements of fault throw or fluid conductivity. Also, the official problem page specifies null/NaN outside training bounds, while this repository’s conservative shared writer emits finite zeros outside the sample footprint; this local-policy difference is unresolved and is another reason the research artifact is not upload-ready. Local [0,1] and grid checks are not proof of organizer acceptance.
