<h2>What we learned (even though verdict negative)</h2>
<ul>
<li>Hand-crafted ridges alone are weak vs learned GB fields: our best hand-crafted 0.0196 barely beats random 0.0184, while GEMSDOE32's 35-channel GB CV field hit 0.125 (peak 0.133 at 88k px) and 0.304 under second protocol.</li>
<li>Transform only raw fields: re-differentiating already-derived products (tc, geod_shearrate) hurts (measured –0.04 to –0.06 AUC), while transforming raw det_elev helps +10% (GEMSDOE32 H62-4 finding, kept).</li>
<li>Catalogue proxy is biased: it cannot reward truly new faults, so holdout is emission-rule validation only; live projection needs identification pack (S1 anchor, S2 scale 0.5, S3 null-add).</li>
</ul>
