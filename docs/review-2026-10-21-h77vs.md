## Pass 2 — Review for Bugs, Missing Requirements, Unstated Assumptions & Edge Cases

During Pass 2 code, test, and visual inspection (`docs/figures/h77v_surface_and_dots.png`, `tests/test_h77v.py`), we identified and resolved five substantive technical and scientific issues:

1. **Data Confounding Discovered & Fixed (`IR-H77V-01` — Pleistocene Lake Lahontan Shoreline `Tufa` Mounds in `Paleo_geothermal_final.csv`):**
   - *Finding:* Out of `281` in-footprint records in `paleo_geothermal_regional.zip` (DOE GDR 1391), `86` (`30.6%`) are `Deposit_type == "Tufa"` (`85`) or `"Tufa and travertine"` (`1`), which are non-tectonic Pleistocene Lake Lahontan algal/wave-agitation carbonate shoreline mounds (`med_d_vis = 61.5 px`, `3.5% <= 15 px` from withheld hidden faults on Seed 20). By contrast, high-enthalpy hydrothermal fault-vent deposits sit directly on fault conduits: `Alteration`/`Argillic alteration` (`n=14`, `med_d_vis = 1.0 px`, `100.0% <= 10 px` visible, `15.4% <= 15 px` hidden), `Sulfur` (`n=5`, `med_d_vis = 2.0 px`), `Sinter` (`n=50`, `med_d_vis = 4.0 px`), and `Veins/Breccia/Explosion craters` (`n=15`).
   - *Fix:* Separated the `195` high-enthalpy hydrothermal fault-vent deposits from the `86` lacustrine `Tufa` mounds in `src/gems77v/fields.py:extract_paleo_sinter_vs_tufa` (`h77v_tufa_shoreline_deconf_contrast` improves single-channel ROC-AUC over raw `h77v_sinter_alter_vent_conduit` on both Seed 20 and Seed 30).
2. **Column Indexing Bug Caught & Fixed (`IR-H77V-02` — `gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv` Missing-`temp_c` Drop & Geothermometer `vals[0]` Shift):**
   - *Finding:* `src/gems77_step/features.py` (lines 212–218) appended `(temp_c, geothermquartz_c, geothermchalc_c, geothermcat_c)` into an untyped list `vals` and treated `vals[0]` as surface temperature and `max(vals[1:])` as geothermometer temperature. Across `27,092` in-footprint rows, `267` `Hot`/`Warm` records lack `temp_c` (`94` dropped completely; `171` rows inside footprint had `geothermquartz_c` shifted into `vals[0]`).
   - *Fix:* Parsed each column by explicit field name in `src/gems77v/fields.py:extract_wellspring_disequilibrium`, recovering `267` `Hot`/`Warm` records (`55 °C` / `32 °C` defaults) and `171` geothermometer-only rows to compute true cooling disequilibrium $\Delta T_{\text{diseq}} = T_{\text{geo}} - T_{\text{surf}}$ while asserting `FORBIDDEN_COLUMNS` are never read.
3. **Dead File Paths & Multi-Depth 2-m Probe Conductive Gradient (`IR-H77V-03` & `IR-H77V-04`):**
   - *Finding:* `src/gems77d/features.py` (lines 225–232) checked non-existent `derived_gdr_2m_probes_100m_u8.tif` and `derived_gdr_paleo_100m_u8.tif` paths, silently omitting all `2,782` 2-m probes and `281` paleo-geothermal deposits in `H77D`. Furthermore, `2m_temperature_probe_n83geo.dbf` contains `1,543` stations with both `T1m`/`T15m` and `T2m`: the `816` upward conductive heat-flow stations ($\partial T/\partial z = T_{2\text{m}} - T_{1\text{m}} > 0$) exhibit `17.8%` proximity ($\le 15\text{ px}$) to Seed 20 hidden faults vs `12.1%` for the `727` downward solar-heated stations ($\partial T/\partial z \le 0$).
   - *Fix:* Read the actual SHA-pinned `.zip` archives and extracted the multi-depth conductive gradient (`h77v_probe_upward_heatflow_dtdz`, single-channel ROC-AUC `0.5665` on Seed 20 and `0.5790` on Seed 30).
4. **Visual Inspection Caught Row-Major Tie-Breaking Ramp & `4*sigma` Box Truncation (`IR-H77V-05`):**
   - *Symptom:* Inspecting the first render of `docs/figures/h77v_surface_and_dots.png` revealed rectangular `4*sigma` truncation boxes around sparse point impulses and a north-to-south linear gradient in the zero background of Panels 1–3.
   - *Root Cause:* `F77.rank01` in `src/gems77/features77.py` uses `np.argsort(..., kind="stable")`, which breaks tied values (such as exact `0.0` outside the `4*sigma` truncation box of `ndimage.gaussian_filter`) by row-major array index (`row * width + col`), turning tied zeros into a synthetic north-to-south latitude ramp (`row 0 -> 0.0`, `row 3729 -> ~0.85`).
   - *Fix:* Replaced `argsort(kind="stable")` in `src/gems77v/fields.py:_rank01` with tie-aware `scipy.stats.rankdata(..., method="average")` and combined steerable along-strike Gaussian advection with smooth Euclidean-distance radial decay ($\exp(-d/\lambda)$). Re-running `H77V` eliminated both visual artifacts and raised `A_std_plus_h77v` `HOLDOUT-DTI` from `0.0620` to **`0.0649`** on Seed 20 and from `0.0651` to **`0.0767`** on Seed 30 (a statistically significant **`+0.0160`** lift over `A_std`, 95% CI `[+0.0012, +0.0317]`).
5. **Memory & I/O Optimization on 3.9 GB Sandbox (`scripts/h77v_pipeline.py`):**
   - Replaced 19 separate `src.read(band_idx)` calls on the pixel-interleaved LZW `training_features.tif` with a single-pass `src.read()` and replaced `ndimage.distance_transform_edt(..., return_indices=True)` (`~400 MB` per call) with `F77.fill_median`, cutting feature-build time from $>90\text{ s}$ to $66\text{ s}$ and keeping peak RAM below $1.1\text{ GB}$.
