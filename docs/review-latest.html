H33: what can and cannot be concluded

The retained forensic record says the H33 mirror has 37,654 positive pixels and that the H33 raster is the 44,090-dot D2.8 mirror with 6,436 near-catalogue dots removed and none added (evidence/prior_forensics.json; raster measurements, not scores). The repo's corrected metric note derives the local Tversky form with α = 0.2, β = 0.8, and the 300 m triangular kernel:

D = α(T + S − M) + βK
s = T / D
new s > old s  iff  ΔT > αs(ΔT + 1 − k)

The simplified k > αs threshold applies only when the new dot's total marginal truth credit is ΔT = k; a dot can add credit to several truth cells, while an overlapping dot may add none. Thus thinning or pruning can plausibly lower redundant prediction mass while preserving much of the maximum-kernel truth credit, but proximity to a mapped trace does not make every removed dot a false positive. The byte lineage is consistent with a sparse/pruned-raster mechanism; it does not prove that this caused the owner's reported 0.2778, that H33 is optimal, or that a new strategy will beat it.
