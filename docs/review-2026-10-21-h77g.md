## Pass 2 — Review for Bugs, Missing Requirements, Unstated Assumptions & Edge Cases

During Pass 2 code, test, and visual inspection (`docs/figures/h77g_surface_and_dots.png`, `tests/test_h77g.py`), we identified and resolved three substantive technical issues:

1. **Bug Caught & Fixed (`IR-H77G-02` — `F77.hessian_ridge` along-axis vs across-axis eigenvalue swap):**
   - *Symptom:* Our unit test `test_hessian_trough_detects_synthetic_linear_magnetic_low` (`tests/test_h77g.py`) tested a straight 1-D Gaussian negative magnetic trough along $x = 20$ and found `F77.hessian_ridge(-hp, sigma=1.5)` returned `6.18e-5` instead of $> 0.10$, and Panel 1 of `docs/figures/h77g_surface_and_dots.png` appeared speckled/isotropic.
   - *Root Cause:* `src/gems77/features77.py:hessian_ridge` computes `big = where(|l1| >= |l2|, l1, l2)`, `small = where(|l1| >= |l2|, l2, l1)`, and returns `-small * (big < 0)`. On a linear ridge, `big` is the across-ridge curvature while `small` is the along-ridge curvature (`small ≈ 0` on a straight 1-D ridge), so `-small * (big < 0)` vanishes on straight ridges and responds to isotropic domes/pits!
   - *Fix:* Left `src/gems77/features77.py` untouched per `AGENTS.md` so closed lanes (`contactphys`, `H79-BB`, `XSEN`) remain byte-reproducible, and implemented the true scale-normalized across-valley principal eigenvalue $\lambda_1 > 0$ weighted by linear tubularity $1 - \min(|\lambda_2|, \lambda_1)/(\lambda_1 + \epsilon)$ in `src/gems77g/fields.py:hessian_trough`. Re-running `H77G` raised `h77g_demag_trough_s1p5` single-feature AUC from `0.5114` to **`0.5347`**, `h77g_demag_persistence` from `0.5066` to **`0.5351`**, `h77g_composite_surface` from `0.5342` to **`0.5618`**, and lifted standalone `S_h77g_physics` `HOLDOUT-DTI` from `0.0355` to **`0.0392`** (Seed 20).
2. **Physical Data Irregularity Discovered (`IR-H77G-01` — 3.5 km / 7.0 km rectilinear mesh periodicity in `cond_surf` Band 17):**
   - *Symptom:* Visual inspection of Panel 3 (`3. MT Conductance Clay-Cap Margin`) in `docs/figures/h77g_surface_and_dots.png` revealed a regular rectilinear grid across the entire Great Basin footprint.
   - *Root Cause:* Direct byte and 1-D FFT audit of Band 17 (`cond_surf`, ingested from the INGENIOUS regional 3-D MT conductance model, USGS Data Release `doi:10.5066/P9TWT2LU`) confirmed that `30.53%` of vertical steps (`dy`) and `30.35%` of horizontal steps (`dx`) are identically zero, and `|∇cond_surf|` at $\sigma = 2.0\text{ px}$ has sharp Fourier peaks at **`35.02 px` (`3,502 m`) and `70.04 px` (`7,004 m`)** along N–S and E–W grid lines. Short-scale gradients ($\sigma < 35\text{ px}$) of `cond_surf` extract the coarse 3.5 km / 7.0 km MT inversion mesh boundaries rather than geological clay-cap edges.
   - *Action:* Logged as `IR-H77G-01` in `docs/irregularities.md` and `knowledge/02_hydrothermal_vent_conduits_great_basin.md` with explicit guidance that future lanes must smooth `cond_surf` at $\sigma \ge 35\text{ px}$ ($3.5\text{ km}$).
3. **Validator Key Schema Alignment (`scripts/h77g_pipeline.py`):**
