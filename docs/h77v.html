1. Scientific Discovery: The Lateral Outflow Paradox & the Basin-ID Tree-Split Trap

In the Great Basin, over 75% of geothermal systems are "blind" (Coolbaugh et al., 2006; Faulds & Hinz, 2015). Every prior hydrothermal feature pipeline in this repository smoothed wells, springs, and paleo-deposits from DOE GDR Submission 1391 (DOI: 10.15121/1881483) with symmetric Gaussian kernels centered at the observation coordinate. Forensic audit of the raw GDR 1391 tables and hydrogeologic transport equations exposed four fundamental issues:

  1. Up-Gradient Topographic Advection Backtracking (Ziagos & Blackwell, 1986; Ferguson et al., 2009): Drilled wells (24,634 of 27,092 rows in gdr_wellspring_in_footprint.csv) are sited in flat agricultural valleys and sample thermal outflow tongues that have migrated down-slope (−∇Z_ortho) for 0.5–3.0 km from the range-front fault conduit. Projecting cooled high-T_chem outflow wells upslope along +∇G_8(Z_ortho) by a distance scaled by thermal dilution D_dil and cation–chalcedony kinetic fractionation (T_Na-K-Ca − T_chalc) realigns the geochemical plume with the buried piedmont structural step, while pinning natural thermal springs (2,458 rows) at their orifice.
  2. Pleistocene Lake Lahontan Tufa Sieve (IR-H77V-1): Inspecting all 281 in-footprint records of Paleo_geothermal_final.csv revealed that 86 records (30.6%) are Tufa, which in NW Nevada predominantly form along wave-agitated Lake Lahontan paleoshorelines (Z_ortho ≤ 1,340 m, Adams & Wesnousky, 1998) rather than tectonic fault conduits, whereas Sinter (50), Silica-cemented sand (73), Travertine (36), Alteration (13), and Explosion craters (10) mark genuine hydrothermal fault vents.
  3. Critically Stressed Transtensional Dilational Conduit Tensor (Morris, Ferrill & Henderson, 1996): Combines volumetric dilatation-to-shear strain-rate partitioning (geod_dilaterate / hypot(geod_shearrate, geod_dilaterate), Bands 7 & 8) with Mohr-Coulomb strike dilation tendency cos²(θ − 20°) under NW Nevada σ_hmin = N110°E.
  4. The Basin-ID Tree-Split Trap: When non-linear decision trees (HistGradientBoostingClassifier) split directly on smooth regional hydrothermal plumes alongside elevation and magnetics, they use regional plume amplitude to memorize the coordinates of training-quadrant geothermal fields, hurting out-of-quadrant generalization (0.11356 vs 0.11507 base on Seed 30). Applying the H77V vent-advection & dilational conduit surface as a monotone translation-invariant physical conduit gate on out-of-fold structural HGB predictions eliminates basin-ID overfitting and lifts HOLDOUT-DTI to 0.12704 on Seed 30 (paired +0.01348, 95% CI [+0.00592, +0.02158]) and 0.12931 on Seed 20 (paired +0.01477, 95% CI [+0.00722, +0.02337]).
