Pass 3 — original request, score interpretation, and safety

What can and cannot be said about the reported H33 0.2778

The value 0.2778 for h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros remains OWNER-REPORT, not an ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED file-to-score receipt. The repository's pinned H33 analysis reports byte forensics consistent with pruning 6,436 near-catalogue pixels from a 44,090-pixel predecessor, leaving 37,654; those are raster measurements, not competition scores. It also records that the GEMSDOE32 page itself states that no organizer score exists for its listed artifact.

A plausible mechanism—not causal proof—is that thinning and near-catalogue pruning reduce redundant prediction mass under an asymmetric distance-weighted Tversky objective while retaining useful coverage of hidden faults. It can help only when the removed mass contributes little to hidden truth; the same operation can hurt if removed dots were useful. The exact marginal-coverage term depends on maximum kernel credit across truth pixels, so there is no generally valid fixed per-dot penalty or universal distance threshold. research/why-h33-scored-02778.md derives the correct condition and tests counterexamples against the pinned implementation.

Therefore: why H33 scored 0.2778 is not established from the evidence available here; the sparse/pruned-field explanation is a defensible hypothesis. Whether a distinct method can beat that owner-reported number is unknown. A holdout improvement is necessary for promotion under the project protocol, but it is neither an organizer leaderboard score nor proof of geothermal productivity. The brief's 0.3195 and repo notes referring to 0.4413 conflict as current targets; no live leaderboard value was verified in this review.
