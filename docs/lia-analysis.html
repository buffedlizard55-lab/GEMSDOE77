Exact marginal condition (including redundancy)

For DTI = T / (α·(T + S − M) + β·K), let k be a candidate dot's nearest-truth kernel weight and dT its marginal increase in weighted true-positive credit. With α+β=1, the exact improvement condition is dT > α·DTI·(dT + 1 − k). Only for a nonredundant dot (dT=k) does it reduce to k > α·DTI.
