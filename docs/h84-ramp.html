1. PhD-Level Diagnosis: Why H33 Scored 0.2778 and How to Score Higher Toward 0.4413

The GEMS Distance-Tolerant Intersection (DTI) metric evaluates a predicted raster $P \in [0,1]^{3730\times 3292}$ across 101 thresholds $q \in \{0.00, 0.01, \dots, 1.00\}$ via a 300 m ($3.0\text{ px}$) triangular distance-decay kernel $K(d)=\max(0,1-d/300\text{ m})$ with $\alpha=0.2$ (recall weight) and $\beta=0.8$ (precision weight). As proven in research/h84ramp_hypotheses.md:

  • Single-threshold quantization ceiling (~0.03–0.06 DTI loss): Because H33 (h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros, 37,553 binary dots) emits values in $\{0,1\}$, all 100 positive thresholds $q \in [0.01, 1.00]$ evaluate the identical binary support $S(q) \equiv D$, collapsing the 101-point threshold integral into a single operating point $(r_0, p_0)$ rather than sweeping high-precision core traces at $q\to 1$ and broader relay/blind corridors at $q\to 0.01$.
  • Single-host distance parameterization ($d_1$ only, ~0.02–0.04 DTI loss): Single-nearest-fault distance $d_1(x,V)$ treats every pixel as controlled by one fault segment. In the Basin and Range, normal faults step en-echelon across 0.3–2.5 km relay ramps where synthetic breaching cross-faults form between two distinct sub-parallel host segments $c_1 \neq c_2$ ($d_1$ and $d_2$ simultaneously moderate, opposite anchor vectors $\langle \hat{u}_1, \hat{u}_2 \rangle < 0$, and co-planar strike $\cos 2\Delta\theta > 0$).
  • Relay-Stepover Self-Collapse Tautology (IR-H84-01): If two-host relay features are evaluated naively against the full visible fault mask $V$, every visible training positive $x \in V$ has $d_1(x,V) \equiv 0$, collapsing 7 of 8 stepover/collar channels identically to 0.0 on 100% of positive training rows (`A_std_plus_h84_naive` drops to 0.0418 s20 / 0.0331 s30). Our 5-bucket Leave-Bucket-Plus-3px-Collar-Out (B-LBCO) operator via bitplane cKDTree removes each segment bucket and its 300 m collar during feature extraction, restoring positive stepover statistics on visible training faults and lifting HOLDOUT-DTI by +0.0948 on Seed 20 and +0.1115 on Seed 30.
