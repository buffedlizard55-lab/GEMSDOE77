How to submit a future approved candidate

The supplied form accepts a single-band GeoTIFF or a ZIP containing one TIFF. The current portal and rules could not be independently re-read here; check the official page before using a real slot.

  1. Wait for explicit selector approval. Require a complete registry clearance, clean canaries, a defensible paired holdout improvement against a fold-rebuilt incumbent, authenticated inputs/rules and a known remaining weekly allowance. This candidate does not qualify.
  2. Download the approved TIFF or its single-TIFF ZIP. Do not submit a webpage, receipt JSON, Markdown, preview image or the entire repository.
  3. Check exact bytes and validator. Match the SHA-256 in its receipt. Require one band, EPSG:32611, shape 3730 × 3292, the sample's geotransform and finite values in [0,1]. These dimensions are measured against the owner mirror, not independently organizer-authenticated.
  4. Open the official competition portal. Use DrivenData GEMS → the competition's submission form after signing in and accepting current rules. The precise current menu may differ. Never share credentials in chat.
  5. Choose the approved file and paste its note. Check the current weekly allowance before pressing the portal's submit control. Do not use the research-only file on this page.
  6. Keep the organizer receipt. Record exact filename/hash, note, UTC timestamp and organizer-reported result. Only then label a result ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED. Local validation is not acceptance.
