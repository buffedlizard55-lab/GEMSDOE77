Why 0.2778, in one paragraph

The 0.2778 file is 37,654 single-pixel dots spaced 3 px apart, all on the H19-5 lineament line set, and none on the mapped catalogue. The metric (problem description) credits a hidden fault if any predicted pixel lies within 300 m (3 px), with linearly decaying weight. It charges 0.2 × every predicted pixel that is not near a fault, and 0.8 × every missed fault pixel. Sparse dots on a line set that runs alongside unmapped faults therefore collect most of the achievable credit at a fraction of the false-positive cost. The binding constraint for every newer file is uniqueness: to be a different submission, at least 30% of the dots must sit more than 3 px from H19-5. No measured field yet earns credit there (H78 E1/E2 and the LIA lane). The full analysis is on Why 0.2778.
