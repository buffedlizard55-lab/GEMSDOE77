Mechanism and alternative explanation

9x9 K/Th local SD, central-difference magnitude, geometric mean, label-free p99 normalization, fixed amplitude 0.02. The inputs are independently quantized rank intensities, not elemental concentrations. A covered fault could separate smooth sediment from heterogeneous bedrock, but a depositional fan-to-lake sediment boundary or acquisition noise can do the same without faulting. No vent, heat source or productive reservoir is identified.
