Forensic answer: why GEMSDOE32 (h33-2-b2-zeros) scored 0.2778 and how to climb toward 0.4413

As derived in docs/lia-analysis.html and research/why-h33-scored-02778.md, the Distance-Tolerant IoU metric uses a 300 m (3 px) linear-decay triangular kernel with $\alpha = 0.2$ false-negative weight and $\beta = 0.8$ false-positive weight ($\beta/\alpha = 4.0$):

  • Why GEMSDOE32 scored 0.2778 (OWNER-REPORT linked to public row #48; top public leaderboard 0.4413 ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED): Rather than painting dense ribbons or continuous probabilities across all 5,167,373 in-footprint cells (which adds millions of $\beta = 0.8$ false-positive penalties while adding zero marginal $\text{TP}$ once truth is covered), GEMSDOE32 emitted exactly 37,654 binary dots spaced by $r_{\min} \approx 3\text{ px}$ along multi-sensor structural lineaments with a 200 m exclusion collar around mapped catalogue faults (`existing_faults.tif`, which are masked out of organizer scoring per competition forum #11516). At $N_{\text{pred}} = 37,654$ and estimated hidden-catalogue size $N_{\text{truth}} \approx 14,500$, achieving $\text{DTI} = 0.2778$ requires only $\text{TP} \approx 9,061$ covered truth pixels ($\text{Recall} \approx 62.5\%$, $\text{Precision} \approx 24.1\%$).
  • Why H77-BGB (`0.058406` standalone / `0.102390` with standard stack + LOCO) does not beat `H77-Step` (`0.152718 HOLDOUT-DTI`) or `H77D` (`0.15011 HOLDOUT-DTI`): The `300 m` (`3 px`) DTI kernel rewards sub-300 m spatial pinpointing of fault traces. Subsurface potential-field (`depth_to_base_surf`, `iso_grav_anom`), geodetic strain (`geod_dilaterate`, `geod_shearrate`), and MT electrical conductivity (`cond_surf`, which has coarse `7 km × 14 km` grid seams documented in `IR-BGB-02`) identify which 2–10 km basin structural domain hosts blind extension or hydrothermal upflow (+0.0032 lift when combined with the 33-channel baseline + LOCO mechanics), but high-resolution 1 m 3DEP LiDAR scarp dipoles (`lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif` with missingness-safe indicators) and 10 m DEM curvature (`H77-Step` and `H77D`) are required to lock the final dots within `<= 300 m` of the fault trace.

Irregularities discovered and logged in this session

  • IR-BGB-01 (Shared Validator CLI Crash on nodata=NaN Twins): vendor/gems57/validate.py converts ds.nodata via float(ds.nodata), producing a Python float('nan') inside meta["nodata"] when validating -nan.tif twins, which caused json.dumps(..., allow_nan=False) in scripts/validate_submission.py to raise ValueError: Out of range float values are not JSON compliant: nan. Fixed in-place in scripts/validate_submission.py via _json_safe() so strict RFC-8259 JSON is always emitted without altering pinned vendor/gems57/ files.
  • IR-BGB-02 (Rectilinear 7 km × 14 km Coarse-Grid Seams in Shipped Band 17 cond_surf): Direct inspection of first differences $\partial_x, \partial_y$ on Band 17 (cond_surf) in training_features.tif reveals periodic step-discontinuity spikes every ~70 columns (7.0 km) and ~140 rows (14.0 km) inherited from coarse magnetotelluric model regridding. Applying a fault-scale ($\sigma = 1.5\text{ px} = 150\text{ m}$) gradient operator $|\nabla\log(1+\text{cond\_surf})|$ extracts this rectilinear grid-seam lattice (visible in Panel 3 of figures/bgb_overview.png), explaining why S_upflow_breach alone scored 0.029188 HOLDOUT-DTI below random (0.048611).
