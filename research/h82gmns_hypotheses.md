### 1.1 Exact Mathematical Anatomy of the Competition Metric (`DTI`)

From the official competition specification ([DrivenData Problem Page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/), [NLR Rules PDF](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf), [Tversky 1977](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tversky_index)) and the pinned reference implementation `vendor/gems57/metric.py` (`SHA-256: 4afe10cbaa229948520c75194b1ebc32676e72544f05582f0725092b097b4d7f`), let $\Omega$ denote the valid study footprint (`5,167,373` `100 m × 100 m` pixels in `EPSG:32611` inside the `3730 × 3292` grid), $G \subset \Omega$ denote hidden unmapped fault pixels (`|G| = K`), and $P \in [0, 1]^\Omega$ denote submitted predictions after masking known USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue traces (`existing_faults.tif`, `59,666` pixels). With isotropic radial triangular kernel $K_3(\delta) = \max\bigl(0, 1 - \|\delta\|_2 / 3\bigr)$ (`3 px = 300 m`):

$$D_P(y) = \max_{\|\delta\|_2 \le 3} K_3(\delta)\,P(y + \delta), \qquad D_G(x) = \max_{\|\delta\|_2 \le 3} K_3(\delta)\,G(x + \delta),$$

$$\mathrm{DTI}(P, G) = \frac{\sum_{y \in G} D_P(y)}{0.8\,|G| + 0.2\,\sum_{y \in G} D_P(y) + 0.2\,\sum_{x \in \Omega} P(x)\bigl(1 - D_G(x)\bigr)}.$$

Direct byte inspection of `GEMSDOE32`'s `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros.tif` (`evidence/why_02778.json`) reveals why this exact raster jumped from `0.0107–0.1922` in earlier repos to `0.2778` (`OWNER-REPORT`):

1. **Elimination of Soft-Probability Background Dilution (`0.0107–0.0439` -> `0.1922`):**
   Because $\mathrm{FP}_{\text{tol}} = \sum_{x} P(x)(1 - D_G(x))$ sums linearly across ~`5.05 × 10^6` off-fault pixels whereas $\mathrm{TP}_{\text{tol}} = \sum_{y \in G} \max_{\|\delta\| \le 3} K_3(\delta) P(y + \delta)$ takes a **pointwise supremum (`max`)**, any smooth continuous probability background $\bar{p} \in [0.02, 0.10]$ adds `20,000–100,000` penalty units to the denominator while contributing zero additional `TP_tol` once a single pixel reaches `1.0`. `H33` emitted strictly binary `{0.0, 1.0}` values (`nodata = None`, zero `NaN`s).
2. **Poisson-Disk Thinning at the Exact Kernel Radius `d = 2.8–3.0 px` (`0.1922` -> `0.2600`):**
   `H19-5` (`19GEMSDOE`, `0.1922 OWNER-REPORT`) emitted `121,131` contiguous ridge pixels. Along a 1D fault trace, adjacent `1.0` pixels overlap inside the `29`-pixel disk $\|\delta\|_2 \le 3$, paying `3x` the false-positive cost for almost identical `max`-kernel coverage. Thinning `H19-5` to isolated binary dots separated by $\approx 2.8\text{ px}$ (`44,090` dots in `GEMSDOE25`/`GEMSDOE30`) cut `FP_tol` by `63.6%` while retaining `>88%` of along-strike kernel integral ($\sum_{t=-1}^{1} K_3(t) = 1 + \frac{2}{3} + \frac{2}{3} = 2.333\text{ TP}_{\text{tol}}$ per isolated dot), lifting live score from `0.1922` to `0.2600` (`OWNER-REPORT`).
