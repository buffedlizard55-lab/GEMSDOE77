## Required gates before any candidate is run

1. Assign one method lane and freeze one hypothesis paragraph; do not tune or reopen a closed lane.
2. Restore only inputs required by that lane and run `python scripts/check_data_ready.py`; this verifies current bytes against the owner mirror, not organizer provenance.
3. Resolve the shared literal 3-px overlap contradiction with the protocol owner. Do not silently ignore full-support registry witnesses or use the informative-subset result as an exemption.
4. Run feature-alone leakage canaries first; AUC above `0.90` is leakage until proven otherwise. Use the pinned template feature stack, whole-segment spatial holdout with buffer, visible-fault-only catalogue features, pixel-exact masking, and pooled DTI (`α=0.2`, `β=0.8`, 300 m triangular kernel).
5. Before placement and on final dots, compare against the authorized registry; stop if absolute rank correlation exceeds `0.90` or more than `70%` of dots overlap.
6. Label every reported score `HOLDOUT-DTI` with evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% CI. Use `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED` only with a submission-page receipt. No projection is a score.
7. Limit a future session to at most three experiments or two hours. Do not select a competition submission here; promotion is a separate selector decision.
