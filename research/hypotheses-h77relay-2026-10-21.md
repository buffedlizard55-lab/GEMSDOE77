Sparse emission + pruning reduces redundant false-positive mass while preserving max-kernel truth coverage (DTI numerator is `max` per truth cell, not sum per dot). Thinning thick ribbons raises credit per pixel toward the marginal bar `k > α·DTI` (α=0.2, bar ≈0.052 at DTI 0.26; exact gain is `ΔT > α·DTI·(ΔT+1−k)`). Pruning near catalogued faults helps if hidden truth is spatially distinct from mapped faults (pixel-exact catalogue masking does not imply halo exclusion). Neither mechanism proves causation, optimality, or a guaranteed leaderboard gain; 0.2778 remains OWNER-REPORT with no organizer receipt (`GEMSDOE32` site states “NO ORGANISER SCORE EXISTS”).
