### 1.2 Why and How the `H33-2-B2` Construction Reached `0.2778`
Direct byte-level inspection (`evidence/prior_forensics.json`, `evidence/why_02778.json`) and the official [Problem Description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) explain the mechanism through three coupled factors:

1. **Exact Distance-Weighted Tversky Index (DTI) Marginal Break-Even Algebra:**
   The official metric uses $\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$, and a 300 m ($R = 3\text{ px}$ at 100 m resolution) linear triangular kernel $k(d) = \max(1 - d/R, 0)$:
   $$\text{DTI}(\alpha, \beta) = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{\text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w + 0.8\,\text{FN}_w}$$
   where $\text{TP}_w = \sum_{g \in G} \max_{x: d(x,g)\le R} p(x)\,k(d(x,g))$, $\text{FP}_w = \sum_{x: p(x)>0} p(x)\,[1 - \max_{g \in G} k(d(x,g))]$, and $\text{FN}_w = |G| - \text{TP}_w$.
   Substituting $\text{FN}_w = |G| - \text{TP}_w$ yields:
   $$\text{DTI} = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{0.2\,(\text{TP}_w + S - M) + 0.8\,|G|}, \qquad S = \sum_x p(x), \quad M = \sum_x p(x)\max_{g \in G} k(d(x,g)).$$
   Taking the finite difference with respect to adding one unit binary dot ($p(x_0)=1$, $\Delta S = 1$) gives the exact break-even inequality:
   $$\Delta \text{DTI} > 0 \iff \Delta \text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{DTI}\cdot(m(x_0) - 1) > 0.$$
   Because $\text{TP}_w$ uses a **hard $\max$** over the 300 m kernel disk around each truth pixel $g \in G$, a second dot placed within $d < 2.8\text{ px}$ of an existing dot covering the same fault strand adds $\Delta \text{TP}_w \approx 0$ while still paying the $\alpha = 0.2$ false-positive mass penalty on the denominator. Converting the dense `H19-5` ridge (`0.1922` `OWNER-REPORT`) into `d2.8` Poisson-disk dots (`44,090` dots, `0.2600` `OWNER-REPORT`) eliminated redundant overlapping mass inside the 300 m kernel.

2. **Catalogue-Collar Pruning ($d(\text{catalogue}) \le 2\text{ px}$ Removed: `44,090` $\to$ `37,654` Dots):**
   As documented on the [Official Problem Page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/), the Initial Prize Round test set consists of **newly identified faults compiled byUSGS/NLR experts that are not contained in the public USGS database**. In `H33-2-B2`, deleting the `6,436` dots lying within $\le 200\text{ m}$ ($2\text{ px}$) of already-mapped catalogue traces removed prediction mass $S$ that sat on already-known segments without uncovering new off-catalogue strands (`0.2600` $\to$ `0.2708` $\to$ `0.2778` `OWNER-REPORT`).

3. **Portal Format Hygiene (`-zeros.tif`, All-Finite `[0, 1]`, `nodata=None`):**
   `training_features.tif` contains `7,113,308` float32 sentinel cells (`-3.4028235e+38`), including `3,061` inside the footprint. Writing predictions with finite `0.0` outside the footprint, `0` NaN cells anywhere, and `nodata=None` prevents the portal's `"Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]"` validator rejection.
