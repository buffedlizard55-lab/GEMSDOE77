This page is a research **hypothesis ranking**, not a leaderboard or a geothermal-vent map. The prompt did not include an identifiable single assigned method paragraph. This session declares **one new narrow lane, H77L: strike-normal, spatially *lagged* Th/K rank-intensity contrast at 3DEP-derived LiDAR micro-scarp crests**. It does **not** rerun the stopped H77 selective-U, H78 lidar-consensus, H77D hydrotectonic or H77-Step ranker lanes. Only H77L is implemented/tested; the alternatives are future separately assigned ideas. Expected improvement is ordinal uncertainty, not a projected score.

**Geophysical caveat:** the cached `ThK` channel is independently percentile-quantized (`uint8`, `0` = missing). No calibration to thorium/potassium concentrations is present. The tested response is a *rank-intensity spatial contrast*, **not** a physical Th/K concentration ratio, inferred K depletion, fluid temperature or geothermal vent.

**Operational blocker:** the pinned owner registry includes a prior continuous raster covering every allowed cell (`ab0a0a62…`); the shared literal overlap gate interprets every positive cell as a dot. This makes any nonempty candidate fail at the surface stage, regardless of how dissimilar its geological ranking is. Do **not** silently skip that raster or claim registry uniqueness; if this fires, stop before point placement, retain only a format-valid **RESEARCH-ONLY** TIFF and report NOT OK TO SUBMIT. A shared protocol/selector policy decision is needed. `0.2778` cannot be credibly beaten by merely relabelling a format-valid research TIFF.
