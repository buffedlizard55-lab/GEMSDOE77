## 1. Forensic Analysis: Why `GEMSDOE32` (`h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros`) Scored `0.2778` (`OWNER-REPORT`) and How to Score Higher

### 1.1 Exact Metric Mechanics ($\text{DTI}$ with $\alpha=0.2, \beta=0.8, R=300\text{ m}$)
Per the official DrivenData problem specification (<https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/>) and NLR technical report (`NLR/TP-5700-96647`, <https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf>), submissions are evaluated on a $100\text{ m}$ UTM Zone 11N grid (`EPSG:32611`, $3730 \times 3292$) via the asymmetric Distance-Weighted Tversky Index:
$$\text{DTI}(P, G) = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{\text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w + 0.8\,\text{FN}_w + \epsilon}, \quad k(d) = \max\!\left(1 - \frac{d}{300\text{ m}},\, 0\right)$$
where $\tilde{G}(x) = \max_{g \in G} k(\|x-g\|_2)$ and $\tilde{P}(x) = \max_{p \in P} k(\|x-p\|_2)$ are $300\text{ m}$ ($3\text{ px}$) radial triangular distance envelopes, $\text{TP}_w = \frac{1}{2}\sum_x (P(x)\tilde{G}(x) + G(x)\tilde{P}(x))$, $\text{FP}_w = \sum_x P(x)(1 - \tilde{G}(x))$, and $\text{FN}_w = \sum_x G(x)(1 - \tilde{P}(x))$.

1. **Why continuous skeleton predictions plateaued at `0.1563–0.1922` (`OWNER-REPORT`)**:
   - `5GEMSDOE` (`0.1563`, `227,507` px) and `16GEMSDOE` (`0.1855`, `123,939` px) and `19GEMSDOE` (`H19-5`, `0.1922`, `121,131` px) emitted contiguous 1-pixel or multi-pixel lines. Because adjacent $100\text{ m}$ pixels share overlapping $300\text{ m}$ triangular cones ($k(100\text{ m}) = 2/3, k(200\text{ m}) = 1/3$), placing predictions on every adjacent pixel along a candidate ridge adds minimal incremental $\tilde{P}(g)$ coverage while multiplying $\text{FP}_w$ by $\sim 3\times$ everywhere the line lies $>300\text{ m}$ from an unmapped test fault.
2. **Why Poisson-disk discretization (`GEMSDOE25`, `0.2600` `OWNER-REPORT`) jumped `+0.0678` DTI**:
   - Sampling the `19GEMSDOE` (`H19-5`) 4-line ridge skeleton into discrete binary dots separated by $d_{\min} = 2.8\text{ px}$ ($280\text{ m}$) reduced total emitted pixels from `121,131` to `~43,400`, cutting false-positive volume $\text{FP}_w$ by nearly two-thirds while preserving $>88\%$ of the $300\text{ m}$ triangular buffer coverage $\tilde{P}(g)$ along true fault strands.
3. **Why catalogue-collar pruning (`GEMSDOE28` `0.2708` $\to$ `GEMSDOE32` `0.2778` `OWNER-REPORT`) added `+0.0178` DTI**:
   - `existing_faults.tif` (`186,087` pixels) is known to the organizer and excluded from the private test set of *new* faults. Removing dots within $d_{\text{cat}} \le 1.0\text{ px}$ (`GEMSDOE28`, `0.2708`) and $d_{\text{cat}} \le 2.0\text{ px}$ ($200\text{ m}$, `GEMSDOE32`, `39,610` dots, `0.2778`) eliminated zero-reward false positives adjacent to mapped faults.
   - Crucially, `GEMSDOE33` (`d_cat <= 3.0 px`, `0.2647` `OWNER-REPORT`) regressed by `-0.0131` DTI, proving that real unmapped tip continuations and splays exist in the $200\text{--}300\text{ m}$ ($2.0 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 3.0\text{ px}$) shell.

### 1.2 Why `GEMSDOE32` (`0.2778`) Trails the Live Leaderboard Top Tier (`#1 xiaofanhu 0.4413`, `#5 0.3539` `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`) and How to Improve

Differentiating `cond_surf` at $<35\text{ px}$ injects rectilinear grid artifacts across basin interiors.
3. **Coupling hydrothermal demagnetization troughs and basement-gravity steps with 3DEP scarps/knickpoints and SLOCO fracture mechanics (`IR-H77G-02`, `IR-H77G-03`)**: `H77G` introduced the corrected across-valley Hessian demagnetization trough (`hessian_trough`) and oriented gravity-basement step (`h77g_grav_base_step`), but evaluated them on the 19-band stack alone with greedy NMS (`0.0619` Seed 20).
