## H33 score attribution and conditional metric mechanism

The family attribution of 0.2778 to the named H33 TIFF remains **OWNER-REPORT**. The archived 2026-10-21 leaderboard note records the same value for participant `extradr19` at row #48, but the row identifies no TIFF or hash; no inspected organizer receipt links those bytes to that row. See [`research/why-h33-scored-02778.md`](why-h33-scored-02778.md) for the complete corrected analysis and evidence classes.

Archived raster forensics describe a H19-5-derived support with Poisson-dot thinning and near-catalogue pruning. Under the recorded DTI, reducing redundant false-positive mass while preserving weighted true-positive credit is a plausible mechanism. This is not a controlled ablation and does not prove that spacing or pruning caused the owner-reported score, that removed dots were false positives, or that any particular geology was detected.

For the metric notation used in the corrected analysis, with `T = TP_w`, `S = emitted mass`, `M = mass on kernel support`, and `K = |G|`,

```text
DTI = T / (0.2·(T + S − M) + 0.8·K)
```

For a new dot with nearest-truth kernel weight `k` and marginal weighted-true-positive gain `dT`, the exact improvement condition is `dT > α·DTI·(dT + 1 − k)`. It reduces to `k > α·DTI` only for a nonredundant dot (`dT = k`). This algebra is conditional; it says nothing about unknown truth at H33's coordinates or why its named file may have received the reported value.

A future method might improve credit efficiency by discovering additional off-catalogue faults, reducing redundant false-positive mass, or both. The evidence here does not establish which change is required, that the geological targets in this preregistration are present in hidden labels, or that any proposed method will improve a score. Local holdout outcomes are **HOLDOUT-DTI** proxy measurements, not leaderboard projections.

## Frozen Three-Experiment Protocol (`registry/preregistration_h77g.json`)

1. **Experiment 1 (`H77G-E1` — Label-Free Feature Construction, Leakage Canaries & Pre-Placement Surface Uniqueness):**
   - Compute the 10 label-free `H77G` channels (`src/gems77g/fields.py`) strictly from `training_features.tif` inside the finite footprint of `sample_submission.tif`.
   - Test every single `H77G` channel alone on the `gems57-pooled-hide-v2` buffered whole-component split (`seed=20`, `11,321` withheld positives; `seed=30`, `11,342` withheld positives). Any channel with symmetric ROC-AUC $> 0.90$ triggers an immediate leakage stop.
   - Run Phase 1 pre-placement surface uniqueness via `vendor/gems57/uniqueness.py` (`compare_array_to_registry`) on `h77g_composite_surface`. Verify `|Spearman ρ| <= 0.90` against all checked rasters.
2. **Experiment 2 (`H77G-E2` — Dual-Seed Spatially Blocked Hide-and-Recover Holdout on `gems57-pooled-hide-v2`):**
   - Evaluate on the unmodified `vendor/gems57/evaluate_holdout.py` (`gems57-pooled-hide-v2`, `alpha=0.2`, `beta=0.8`, `R=300 m` triangular kernel, `B=37,654` dots, `> 3 px` from visible catalogue, 300 m submodular cover `allocate_submodular_300m`) across both `Seed 20` (`11,321` withheld positives) and `Seed 30` (`11,342` withheld positives):
     - `S_h77g_physics`: pure `H77G` composite physics surface (no catalogue training),
     - `A_h77g_only`: 4-fold OOF `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` trained on the 10 `H77G` channels alone,
     - `A_std_plus_h77g`: 4-fold OOF `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` trained on the 19 standard bands + 10 `H77G` channels (`29` channels),
     - `A_std`: 4-fold OOF `HistGradientBoostingClassifier` trained on the 19 standard bands alone (matched control),
     - `A0_random`: uniform random surface with identical allocator and budget.
3. **Experiment 3 (`H77G-E3` — Full-Map Unique GeoTIFF Packaging, Final-Dot Uniqueness & Independent Validation):**
   - Emit `B = 37,654` discrete dots (`> 3 px` from mapped catalogue) from the `H77G` lane (`A_h77g_only` 4-quadrant out-of-fold surface blended with the preregistered `S_h77g_physics` composite so predictions remain strictly inside the `H77G` lane without collapsing onto standard-stack tree dots).
   - Write `submissions/gems77-h77g-demagtrough-gravstep-37654dots-20261021-<hash8>-zeros.tif` (and `.zip`, `.json`, plus copies in `docs/downloads/`) via the unmodified `vendor/gems57/submission_writer.py`.
   - Run Phase 2 final-dot uniqueness (`compare_array_to_registry`) against the checked registry (including pinned `dense_witness` `ab0a0a62`, `H33` `c55bafc4`, 32 scored rasters $\ge 0.14$, and all local `GEMSDOE77` sibling TIFFs) and run `scripts/validate_submission.py`.
   - Record the complete JSON run card at `evidence/run_card_h77g.json` (and `docs/evidence/run_card_h77g.json`). Unless the candidate beats the `0.152718 HOLDOUT-DTI` incumbent with a positive 95% paired CI *and* clears literal uniqueness *and* receives human selector approval, the verdict is **`negative`** and the file is marked **`OK TO DOWNLOAD FOR INSPECTION; NOT OK TO SUBMIT`**.
