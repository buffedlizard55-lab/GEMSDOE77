## Selected test (frozen before E1)

**H82SE-STRIKE-ENTROPY.** For each holdout seed, start from the shared evaluator's `visible` fault raster only. Smooth its binary mask at σ=1 px, take first derivatives, then average the gradient structure tensor over σ=10 px. Compute `H = 1 - sqrt((Jxx-Jyy)^2 + 4*Jxy^2)/(Jxx+Jyy+ε)` and multiply by `1-exp(-local_density / q95(local_density))`, where `local_density` is a σ=10 px Gaussian of visible-fault pixels and `q95` is measured on footprint cells. Normalize by the footprint maximum to [0,1]. No hidden component, full-catalogue geometry, labels-as-features, or other raster enters the arm.

E1: test this sole feature as a leakage canary (symmetric AUC ceiling 0.90), then compare the full-catalogue surface against the indexed public owner-repository inventory before placement (absolute Spearman ceiling 0.90). Any breach stops the lane. E2: fixed-budget 37,654-dot candidate plus random and visible-distance controls on seed 20 and seed 30; use `gems57-pooled-hide-v2`, 300 m triangular kernel, α=0.2, β=0.8, whole-component quadrant hide-and-recover, 3-pixel context buffer, exact visible-fault pixel masking, and paired 20 km spatial-block bootstrap. No hyperparameter tuning. E3: only if E1 passes, build an all-catalogue, catalogue-excluded TIFF with the shared fail-closed writer, then rerun final-dot uniqueness; a duplicate or incomplete registry scan forces `negative` / `NOT OK TO SUBMIT`. No weekly slot may be used by this lane.

The minimum separation and dot budget are operational comparisons, not claims about the organizer's unknown hidden-fault density. HOLDOUT-DTI is a local proxy and not an organizer score.
